1. The service

Hulhul (the "app") is an iOS app that finds photos of a person you choose in your photo library and helps you delete or hide the photos you review and select. It is provided by ForgeLab (Representative Jason Lee).

2. Responsibility for deletion

  • The app never deletes photos on its own. Every deletion happens only after you review and select photos and approve it in the iOS confirmation prompt.
  • Face matching can make mistakes. Please check the results on the review screen before deleting.
  • Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days and can be recovered during that time. After that they cannot be recovered, and this is your responsibility.
  • Deleting photos from an iCloud Shared Library or shared album can affect the people you share it with. The app warns you and leaves them out by default, but choosing to include them is your responsibility.

3. Free use, purchases and refunds

The app is free to download. For free, you can search and review without limit and clear (let go of or seal) up to 10 photos. Buying Hulhul Pro (a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription) removes the limit on clearing and unlocks every feature, including searching originals stored only in iCloud. The free range and the price are shown in the app and on the App Store; any change will be announced on this page.
