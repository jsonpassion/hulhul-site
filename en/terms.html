1. The service

Hulhul (the "app") is an iOS app that finds photos of a person you choose in your photo library and helps you delete or hide the photos you review and select. It is provided by ForgeLab (Representative Jason Lee).

2. Responsibility for deletion

  • The app never deletes photos on its own. Every deletion happens only after you review and select photos and approve it in the iOS confirmation prompt.
  • Face matching can make mistakes. Please check the results on the review screen before deleting.
  • Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days and can be recovered during that time. After that they cannot be recovered, and this is your responsibility.
  • Deleting photos from an iCloud Shared Library or shared album can affect the people you share it with. The app warns you and leaves them out by default, but choosing to include them is your responsibility.

3. Free use, purchases and refunds

The app is free to download. For free, you can search and review without limit and clear (let go of or seal) up to 50 photos in total. To clear more, you can buy one of the following.

  • Hulhul Pro subscription (monthly or yearly, auto-renewable): removes the limit on clearing and unlocks searching originals stored only in iCloud. The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and payment is charged to your Apple Account. You can cancel anytime in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions; after canceling you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • 100 Photos Pack (consumable, one-time purchase): each purchase adds 100 photos you can clear. The remaining count is stored only on the device you bought it on, does not move to other devices, and cannot be restored through the App Store. It may remain in that device's keychain after you delete and reinstall the app.

The free range and the prices are shown in the app and on the App Store; any change will be announced on this page.

Payments and refunds follow Apple's Media Services Terms and Conditions and the App Store refund policy.
