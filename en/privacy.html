Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Poof (the "app") is provided by ForgeLab (Representative Jason Lee). In short: the app does not collect any personal information. Face data is processed and stored only on your device (see section 3). 한국어: 개인정보 처리방침

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no server, no account and no analytics tools. It does not collect or send your name, email, location, advertising identifier or anything else.

2. Photos

  • The app accesses your photo library only as far as you allow, for one purpose: finding photos of the person you choose.
  • Photos you clear go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app after iOS asks you to confirm, and stay there for 30 days. Photos you seal are moved to Hidden in the Photos app.

3. Face data

The app uses face data only to find photos of the person you choose. All face processing happens on your device, and face data never leaves it.

3.1 What face data the app creates

  • When you pick one to three photos of a person, the app finds the face in each photo with Apple Vision and turns it into a face feature vector (a list of 512 numbers) with a machine learning model built into the app. The photos you pick and these reference vectors are kept in memory only while you use the app and are never saved.
  • During a search, the app creates the same kind of vector for each face it finds in your photo library.
  • The app does not save face images, does not attach names or any identity to faces, and does not use Face ID or the TrueDepth camera.

3.2 How face data is used

  • Only to compare the faces in your photos with the person you chose, so the app can show you that person's photos.
  • It is never used for advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, identifying who someone is, or training models.

3.3 Where face data is stored

  • Only on your device, inside the app's private storage. To make the next search faster, the app keeps (a) the face vectors computed for each photo, linked to the photo's on-device identifier, and (b) one summary vector of the person you searched for last.
  • This data is excluded from iCloud and device backups, and it is never uploaded. The app has no server.

3.4 Sharing

  • Face data is not shared with anyone: not with the developer, not with Apple, and not with any third party. The app contains no third-party SDKs.

3.5 Retention and deletion

  • Face data is deleted automatically 30 days after your last search. The app checks this each time it opens.
  • When you search for a different person, all stored face vectors are deleted and computed again.
  • When you clear photos with the app, the face vectors for those photos are deleted right away.
  • You can delete all face data at any time with Settings > Clear Analysis Records in the app. Deleting the app also deletes all of it.

3.6 Your consent

  • Before you pick a face for the first time, the app explains how face data is handled and asks for your agreement. You can read it again at any time in Settings > About Face Data.

4. Settings stored on your device

The app stores a few values on your device, such as how many photos you have cleared for free and how many pack photos remain (numbers), when your subscription expires, and the app language. The clearing counts and the IDs of pack purchases already added are also kept in your device's keychain, so they may remain after you delete and reinstall the app. None of these values leave your device.

5. Payments

The Poof Pro subscription and the 100 Photos Pack are sold through Apple App Store in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment, and the app never sees your payment details. Refunds follow Apple's policies.

6. Sharing with third parties

Since nothing is collected, nothing is shared.

7. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page. Important changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
