Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026

Hulhul (the "app") is provided by ForgeLab (Representative Jason Lee). In short: the app does not collect any personal information. 한국어: 개인정보 처리방침

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no server, no account and no analytics tools. It does not collect or send your name, email, location, advertising identifier or anything else.

2. Photos and face data

  • The app accesses your photo library only as far as you allow, for one purpose: finding photos of the person you choose.
  • Face matching runs entirely on your device using Apple Vision and a machine learning model built into the app. No photo and no face data ever leaves your device.
  • To make later searches faster, the app stores the computed face features (numeric vectors) and one summary feature of the last person you searched, on your device only. You can erase them at any time with "Clear Analysis Records" in the app's Settings, and they are removed when you delete the app.
  • Photos you clear go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app after iOS asks you to confirm, and stay there for 30 days. Photos you seal are moved to Hidden in the Photos app.

3. Settings stored on your device

The app stores a few values on your device, such as how many photos you have cleared for free and how many pack photos remain (numbers), when your subscription expires, and the app language. The clearing counts and the IDs of pack purchases already added are also kept in your device's keychain, so they may remain after you delete and reinstall the app. None of these values leave your device.

4. Payments

The Hulhul Pro subscription and the 100 Photos Pack are sold through Apple App Store in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment, and the app never sees your payment details. Refunds follow Apple's policies.

5. Sharing with third parties

Since nothing is collected, nothing is shared.

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page. Important changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.

8. Contact

If you have questions, send them with the button below. We will reply as soon as we can.
